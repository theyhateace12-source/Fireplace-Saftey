Fireplace Safety

A simple step-by-step guide for starting, safely burning, and putting out a wood fire.

⚠️ Most important rule: Never leave a burning wood fire unattended. Do not leave the house or go to sleep while it is burning.

🔥 Starting the Fire

Follow these steps in order.

  1. Make sure the damper/flue is OPEN.
  2. Put ONE Duraflame Quick Start firelighter on the metal grate. Break off ONE piece only; do not use the whole 4-pack.
  3. Put 1–2 pieces of dry firewood around/above it, leaving air gaps. Do not cover it completely.
  4. Light the rough-textured edge with a long lighter or fireplace match.
  5. When the wood catches well, add larger pieces one at a time.
  6. Keep the mesh screen CLOSED while burning, except briefly when adding wood.
Remember: The fire needs airflow. Don't bury the firelighter or pack the wood tightly together.

🛡️ Safety Rules

  • Never leave a burning wood fire unattended.
  • Do not leave the house or go to sleep while it is burning.
  • Keep the damper FULLY OPEN while flames, smoke, or hot coals are present.
  • Never add gasoline, lighter fluid, alcohol, or other liquids.
  • A mesh screen contains sparks/embers but does NOT make an unattended fire safe.
  • Flames going out does not mean the fire is safe; hot embers can remain for hours.
Damper warning: Never close the damper while smoke or hot coals are still present. Closing it can allow dangerous smoke and carbon monoxide to enter the room.

🧯 Putting the Fire Out

The safest approach is to let the fire burn down naturally.

  1. Stop adding wood.
  2. Keep the mesh screen closed.
  3. Keep the damper open while burning or producing smoke.
  4. Let the wood burn down naturally; avoid moving burning logs unnecessarily.
  5. When only glowing embers remain, gently spread them into a thin layer with the poker.
  6. Leave the damper OPEN until the fire and embers are completely out and no longer producing smoke.
  7. When everything is completely cold, put ashes in a metal container with a tight-fitting lid, outside and away from the house.
Do NOT pour a large amount of water onto a hot fireplace unless it is an emergency. Sudden cooling can damage masonry and create steam.

❓ If You're Unsure

🛑 Not sure the damper is open?
Do not light the fire until you have confirmed it is open.
📖 Instructions differ?
Follow your fireplace/chimney and firelighter manufacturer's instructions if they differ.

🚨 Emergency

If smoke enters the room, the fire behaves unexpectedly, or you suspect a chimney/fire problem:

Move to safety and contact emergency/fire services as appropriate.

Keep a working carbon-monoxide alarm near the fireplace/living area.
